## Reads

**Only lr_shift reopens.** It is the LR-segment dissection instrument, not a
Tier-1 method — so the honest framing is: *none of the four Tier-1 interventions
(reset/spike/freeze/cram) reopened arithmetic at d50, but the dissection reveals
why.*

**Contrast A (lr_shift vs null) — POSITIVE, the main finding.** Replaying the
early LR schedule *segment* [0,2500) across the late arith window recovers 84%
of the gap with no forgetting cost (ppl actually improved). → the d50 arith
critical period is **largely LR-schedule (optimizer-plasticity) closure, not
representational / data-order entrenchment.** The delayed model's weights are
not "locked in"; they just never got the high-LR budget arithmetic needs,
because LR had decayed by the time arith entered the stream.

**Contrast B (reset+spike ≈ reset, both ~0.58).** A brief spike does NOT rescue
reset → reset's partial (+27%) help is not an LR-starvation artifact; embedding
reset is simply the wrong lever (the arith circuit lives in the blocks/lm_head,
not the input embedding). The pre-registered reset×LR confound is thus resolved
in the *negative* direction: no hidden LR artifact is masking a good reset.

**spike (brief, 0.536) ≪ lr_shift (sustained, 0.752).** It is not merely "high
LR late" — a 300-step bump barely moves the needle. The *sustained* early-LR
regime is what recovers. Arithmetic re-acquisition needs an optimization-budget
regime, not a transient.

**freeze is actively harmful (0.332, −60%).** Only-embedding-trainable starves
the components that actually compute arithmetic; it degrades below the untouched
null.

**Per-band:** lr_shift recovers even the hardest band (4-digit-carry 0.605 vs
null 0.278). freeze collapses it (0.185). No aggregate is masking a hard-band
failure for the winning arm.

**cram (0.574):** per the pre-registered stats rule, NOT a peer — dose-capped at
+22.5% relative by d50's already-dense stream. A +23% recovery at max feasible
dose is uninformative about "does concentrated re-exposure reopen." Covariate
only.

## Prediction check

Prediction #2 (embedding/layer reset = most effective intervention) is **NOT
borne out**: reset flagged for forgetting and fell short of the gate; the
LR-segment instrument won. Implication for Week 8: if the lever is LR schedule
rather than reset, the recovered circuit is expected to be the *same* one
re-plasticized, not a *new* circuit (reset's "new circuit" pathway is moot here).

Directionally as predicted (early exposure + decayed LR -> arith collapses),
BUT **confounded and NOT clean confirmation**: imposing the decayed LR on the

69.2) — the early window doubles as the from-scratch base-LM phase, so stripping
its warmup+peak degrades the whole model, and arith rides on a broken substrate.
The two directions are ASYMMETRIC: the forward lr_shift arm had a HEALTHY base
LM already built (steps 0-2500 under warmup+peak) before its intervention
window, so it isolated the arith-window LR cleanly; the converse cannot. A clean
converse isn't easily constructible (early arith exposure necessarily overlaps
the base-LM-building phase).

=> The forward lr_shift arm remains the clean evidence. The Q2 negative control
and Q3 weight-state control now carry MORE of the confirmation burden.

## Controls — RAN (seed 1, 2026-07-21). Both favor the arith-specific LR read.

**Q1 cram_lrshift = 0.746** (refs: null 0.506, lr_shift alone 0.752, cram alone
0.574). Adding cram's density boost on top of the restored LR added NOTHING
(0.746 ~ 0.752, marginally lower). Decomposition:
  - restore LR at base density 0.40:        0.506 -> 0.752  (+0.246)
  - add cram density under decayed LR:       0.506 -> 0.574  (+0.068)
  - both together:                           0.746  (~= LR alone)
=> **cram's failure was LR-STARVATION, not the dose cap.** At d50 the binding
constraint is the LR schedule, not the amount of arith data; density is a weak
lever that saturates once LR is restored. This CORRECTS the "dose-capped
covariate" framing — even uncapped density (cram+LR) doesn't beat LR-alone.
STATS note (Week 9): report cram's real failure mode as LR-starvation, not just
the +22.5% dose ceiling.

**Q2 negative control = NO recovery (arith-specific confirmed).** lr_shift on
`syntax_delay50_exp10_size23M_seed1@2500` (delayed skill, NO critical period).
Proper continuation metric (gap_region_interaction.py, step 5000):
  - null:      local 0.653, LD 0.545
  - +lr_shift: local 0.629, LD 0.524   (both slightly DOWN)
General val_ppl IMPROVED (47.7) -> the high-LR window WAS optimizing, it just
did not lift syntax. So the identical manipulation reopened arith (+0.246) but
not syntax. **The 84% is arithmetic-specific, not "late LR helps anything
under-trained."**
  - CAVEAT: syntax LD has no critical-period gap (delayed ~ delay-0, ~0.52-0.55),
    so this tests "lr_shift doesn't manufacture a skill from nothing" more than
    "lr_shift doesn't reopen an under-trained-WITH-GAP non-CP skill." The latter
    is untestable here because arith is the ONLY skill with a demonstrated CP
    gap. Limitation, not fatal — the skill-specific dissociation (arith up,
    syntax flat, general LM up) still holds.
